TITLE: SMB SERVICE PACKAGE TEMPLATES (INTERNAL – NOT FOR CLIENTS)

Purpose: speed up solution design by having three reference offers.

BRONZE – BASIC STABILITY
- Target:
  - Very small offices (ten or fewer users).
  - Cost-sensitive customers.
- Components:
  - Single SMB firewall or router.
  - Single L2 switch.
  - One or two access points.
  - Single NAS with RAID 1 (two disks).
  - Basic UPS for router, switch, and NAS.
- Features:
  - Guest Wi-Fi VLAN.
  - Central shared folder on NAS.
  - Basic NAS snapshot backup plus cloud sync of critical folders.
- Limitations:
  - No high availability; hardware failure means downtime until repair.
  - Recovery time and recovery point objectives are not guaranteed below several hours.

SILVER – GROWING SMB
- Target:
  - Ten to forty users, more critical data.
- Components:
  - Business-grade firewall with VPN and basic security features.
  - L2 or L3 core switch plus PoE for access points and phones.
  - Two to three access points with controller functionality.
  - Either:
    - One virtualization host plus NAS with better performance, or
    - Two smaller hosts with shared storage or good backup.
  - UPS solution sized for at least fifteen to twenty minutes runtime of core gear.
- Features:
  - VLANs for staff, guest, management, and VoIP.
  - Central Active Directory or identity, or strong integration with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.
  - Proper backup with three-two-one rule and cloud component.
- Limitations:
  - Some downtime still expected on hardware failures.
  - Not a full high-availability cluster.

GOLD – HIGHER AVAILABILITY SMB
- Target:
  - More than thirty users, or high downtime cost.
- Components:
  - Redundant firewall pair if budget allows.
  - Two or more virtualization hosts.
  - Shared storage or at least very fast backup or replication between hosts.
  - Larger UPS with better runtime and graceful shutdown orchestration.
- Features:
  - Shorter recovery time objective (aim for less than one hour on most incidents).
  - More advanced monitoring and alerting.
  - Stronger security posture (endpoint detection and response, more granular segmentation).
- Limitations:
  - Requires bigger budget and more careful maintenance.

Usage
- Use these packages as starting templates only.
- Always adjust to real answers from the discovery checklist.
- Internally, tag designs as Bronze, Silver, or Gold to estimate effort quickly and align expectations.
