TITLE: SMB DISCOVERY CALL CHECKLIST (INTERNAL USE ONLY)

Goal: collect enough information in one call to design a decent small office solution.

1. Company profile
- What does your company do?
- How many employees total, and how many in the office versus remote?
- Any planned growth in the next two to three years?

2. Current pain points
- What annoys people most about your current IT setup?
  - Slow Wi-Fi?
  - Files disappearing?
  - Frequent downtime?
- Any recent incidents such as ransomware or hardware failures?

3. Applications and workloads
- Key applications:
  - Office suite, email, accounting or ERP, CRM, design tools, and so on.
- Are these:
  - Local (server in office)?
  - Cloud (software as a service)?
- Any special applications needing low latency to a server?

4. Data and storage
- Roughly how much shared data do you have (documents, projects, and so on)?
  - Less than one terabyte, one to five terabytes, more than five terabytes?
- How fast is it growing?
- Any regulatory requirements such as GDPR or industry-specific rules?

5. Network and connectivity
- Current internet connection:
  - Provider, speed, static IP yes or no.
- Any branches or warehouses connected via VPN?
- Do you use VoIP phones and if so, how many?

6. Devices
- How many:
  - Desktops?
  - Laptops?
  - Phones or tablets used for work?
  - Barcode scanners or other special devices?

7. Availability and budget
- How bad is downtime for you:
  - Annoying but survivable versus losing a lot of money per hour?
- How long can you be down before it is a real problem?
  - One to two hours, one day, multiple days?
- Budget range (even rough) for improvements:
  - Low, medium, high; define ranges internally, for example low is less than five thousand euros, medium is five to fifteen thousand euros.

8. Security and backup
- Do you have a backup system today and when was the last successful restore test?
- Any previous security incidents?
- Are there any compliance audits you must pass?

9. Future plans
- More remote work?
- New locations or moving to a different office?
- Plans to migrate more services to cloud?

10. Decision process
- Who is the final decision maker?
- What is their main concern (price, stability, security, vendor neutrality, and so on)?
